Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deliveries, freshness guarantees, and custom arrangements.

Home Help & FAQ
What is the cutoff time for same-day flower delivery?

Our daily cutoff time for guaranteed same-day delivery is 2:00 PM local time from Monday through Saturday. Orders placed after 2:00 PM will be freshly arranged and hand-delivered early the following morning.

How does your 7-day freshness guarantee work?

We harvest directly from partner eco-growers at dawn and keep stems continuously nourished in hydration pods. If your flowers do not stay radiantly fresh for 7 days, simply email us a photo at concierge@florabloom.com. We will send an immediate free replacement or issue a 100% refund.

Can I customize the stems or request specific colors?

Yes! Our artisan florists love bespoke requests. You can add specific color palette preferences or flower substitutions in the order notes during checkout, or connect with our concierge directly via telephone or our contact page.

Do all bouquets include a vase and a personalized card?

Every single bouquet arrives with a complimentary handwritten linen card with your personalized greeting. Many of our featured collections include a luxury ceramic vase as indicated on the product page, and you can also add bespoke vases to any order during checkout.

What happens if the recipient is not home during delivery?

If the recipient is not home, our courier specialist will place the box in a safe, shaded location (porch, concierge, or friendly neighbor) and send an immediate photo confirmation via SMS. If no safe location exists, we will re-attempt delivery at no extra charge.

How should I care for my flowers upon arrival?

Trim 2-3 cm off each stem at a 45° angle under running water, place in a clean vase with room-temperature water mixed with the included flower food packet, and remove any leaves that sit below the waterline. Visit our Flower Care Guide for full detailed tips.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay with 256-bit SSL encrypted checkout security.

Still Have Questions?

Our botanical concierge team is on standby 7 days a week to help with custom orders, wedding decor, or live dispatch tracking.
