Science of Freshness

Why Our Blooms Outlast The Rest

We have reimagined the botanical cold chain to deliver flowers at the peak of their vitality.

Dawn Harvesting

Flowers are cut at first daylight when cellular hydration pressure is at its peak, locking in moisture and vibrant petal pigmentation.

Climate Controlled Fleet

Our dedicated delivery vans are maintained at a precise 10°C to 12°C, keeping flowers peacefully dormant until unboxed in your home.

Bio-Hydration Pouches

Every bouquet travels with stem ends submerged in an organic nutrient gel that feeds water and sugars continuously for up to 48 hours.
