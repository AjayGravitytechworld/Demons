Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about deliveries, vase life, and order changes.

Do you offer same-day flower delivery?

Yes! For all orders placed before 2:00 PM local time (Monday through Saturday), we guarantee hand-delivered same-day arrival using temperature-controlled courier vehicles.

How does the 7-day freshness guarantee work?

We source directly from eco-gardens without intermediary warehouses. If your stems fail to bloom radiantly for 7 full days, simply snap a picture and message us; we will issue a free replacement bouquet or full refund immediately.

Can I customize the flower arrangement and write a card?

Every bouquet comes with a complimentary handwritten linen greeting card. You can write your custom message during checkout or contact our concierge for bespoke stem substitutions.

How are the flowers protected during transit?

Each bouquet is stems-submerged in our proprietary biodegradable hydration pouch that feeds nutrients continuously for up to 48 hours, cushioned in breathable protective kraft paper.
